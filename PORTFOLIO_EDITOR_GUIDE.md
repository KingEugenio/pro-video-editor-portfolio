# CineFolio — Video Editor's Portfolio & CMS Guide

Welcome to your **CineFolio Video Editor Portfolio Platform**. This platform is designed specifically for video editors, colorists, filmmakers, and motion designers to effortlessly showcase their work, manage multi-orientation cuts (16:9 & 9:16), stream directly from Google Drive, and receive client bookings.

---

## 🚀 Quick Start in 3 Minutes

1. **Accessing Your CMS Dashboard:**
   - Click the **"CMS Studio"** button in the top navigation bar or navigate to `/admin` (or add `?admin=true` to your URL).
   - Enter your administrator password (default password: `admin123`).

2. **Launch the 12-Step Setup Wizard:**
   - In the left sidebar, click **"12-Step Setup Wizard"**.
   - Fill in your name, professional title, location, bio, editing software, and paste your showreel link.
   - Click **"Launch Portfolio"** to instantly update your public website.

---

## 🎬 Managing Projects & Multi-Orientation Videos

### Adding a New Project
1. In the admin sidebar, click **"Projects Library"** → **"+ Add New Project"**.
2. **Project Details:** Enter the Project Title, Client, Release Year, Category, Role, and Brief narrative.
3. **Videos & Orientations:**
   - Click **"+ 16:9 Landscape Video"** for master cinema/commercial cuts.
   - Click **"+ 9:16 Vertical Reel"** for high-retention TikTok, Instagram Reel, or YouTube Shorts cuts.
   - CineFolio supports Direct MP4 URLs, Google Drive video streams, YouTube, and Vimeo.
4. **RAW vs Graded Comparison:**
   - In the **Before/After Color Grade** tab, provide links for the raw log frame vs the graded master frame to enable the interactive slider on your project case study page.
5. Click **"Create & Publish Project"**.

---

## 📁 Streaming Videos Directly from Google Drive

CineFolio includes first-class Google Drive streaming so you never have to re-encode or compress your final cuts.

1. Navigate to **"Google Drive Video Hub"** in the sidebar.
2. Click **"Connect Google Workspace / Drive"** or use the **"Add Drive Link"** button.
3. Once linked, you can:
   - Click **"New Project"** next to any drive file to turn it into a case study.
   - Click **"Set Showreel"** to make that video your main homepage showreel.

---

## 🎨 Brand Studio & Live Color Customization

Want to change your portfolio's aesthetic or typography?

1. Go to **"Brand Studio & Tokens"**.
2. Select from curated palettes (e.g. *Electric Cyan & Dark Cinema*, *Obsidian & Gold Luxury*, *Neon Violet Cyber*, *Monochrome Editorial*), or pick any custom HEX color.
3. Select your font pairings (e.g. *Syne*, *Playfair Display*, *Space Grotesk*, *Cinzel*).
4. Use the **Live Device Preview** (Desktop, Tablet, Mobile) on the right side to watch your changes update instantly.

---

## 📬 Receiving Client Bookings & WhatsApp Leads

1. Go to **"Booking Form Config"**.
2. Enter your **Formspree Endpoint** (e.g., `https://formspree.io/f/your_form_id`).
3. Toggle on **WhatsApp Direct Chat** and enter your mobile number (including country code, e.g., `+14155552671`).
4. All incoming commission briefs will arrive in your **"Client Enquiries Inbox"** and also forward directly to your email.
